The runtime reads Segoe UI Emoji installed with Windows. No Microsoft or
Apple emoji font is redistributed by Tide. Emoji.Wpf also includes its
own flag fallback drawings. Unicode emoji sequence data is used to
recognize sequences; see Unicode-LICENSE.txt.

Tide's emoji-sequences.json is derived from Unicode 17.0 emoji-test.txt,
Copyright 2025 Unicode, Inc. Sources:
https://github.com/unicode-org/unicodetools/blob/main/unicodetools/data/emoji/17.0/emoji-test.txt
https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr51/
It retains fully-qualified entries and accepted ZWJ/keycap variants, sorted
longest first. Default-text characters such as bare arrows stay text.
